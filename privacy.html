Capybar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Capybar ("the game") is a casual capybara bar-management game made by an independent developer. This page explains what information is used when you play.

Information the game itself collects

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location, and the developer does not run any servers that collect your data. Your game progress (coins, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The mobile versions of the game show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game bonus. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device type, operating system, language, and app interaction data related to ads.

Google uses this information as described in its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone you can control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
